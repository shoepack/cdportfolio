Homepage of jasonartley.com (the whole site). Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, action
Recruiters and hiring managers screening on phones from LinkedIn; peer interviewers reading closer. Action: reach out by email or LinkedIn, or open the resume PDF.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The category standard, played straight and executed at full fidelity: a calm, light, typographic personal site whose proof is real, public work. Refuses the dark dev-portfolio template (glow, gradient title, logo wall) and any themed costume.
OWN-WORLD: Warm off-white paper #f7f6f2, near-black ink, one secondary grey, one accent: pine #1f6b45 taken from the portrait's foliage. Schibsted Grotesk for everything; Red Hat Mono only for dates and numbers. Hairline rules, pine-wash icon tiles (Feather), brand marks only on tool chips, real logos on jobs.
STORY: In one screen: Jason Artley, Solutions Architect, San Diego; one proof sentence led by the strongest claim: sole developer of reotempintelligence.com (first Stripe subscription), plus the AI tools; Resume, Email, LinkedIn. Then the work itself, then how he did it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Text nav (Work, Experience, Contact, Resume). Name as h1, role line, proof sentence, a full-width pine Resume button with Email and LinkedIn beside or below, all inside 844px on a phone; portrait beside on desktop, below the actions on phones.
FORM: Canon (the standing exit), chosen by Jason after comparing three built directions. seed:12619c66cff89c54
SIGNATURE: Work in public: a React Bits Pro Browser Window clicking through live screenshots of reotempintelligence.com and wakefrequencycalc.com (phone captures on phones). Motion: the hero settles in once on load; nothing else animates on scroll.
